Findings¶

  • Most vessels are recorded as In navigazione a motore (moving under engine power).
  • Smaller numbers of vessels are Ormeggiata (moored), Alla fonda (at anchor), In pesca (fishing), or In navigazione a vela (under sail).
  • Most vessels are actively moving.

Note:

  • Moored = tied to a dock, pier, or buoy.
  • At anchor = stationary using an anchor instead of being tied to a dock.

Findings¶

  • The Tracks dataset contains 1,196 vessels, while Positions contains 1,178.

  • They have little or no AIS position data and appear to be incomplete records.
  • they can be removed without affecting ETA prediction
